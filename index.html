daily-journal

daily-journal is a personal tool that turns short video clips filmed each day into a dated YouTube Short, uploads it to its owner's own YouTube channel and sends it to the owner's TikTok inbox as a draft to post from the app.

It is used only by its owner. It asks Google for permission to upload videos and manage playlists on the owner's YouTube channel, and TikTok for permission to send videos to the owner's TikTok inbox (video.upload), and uses them only to publish the owner's journal videos.
