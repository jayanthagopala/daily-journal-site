daily-journal is a personal, single-user tool run by its owner on their own home server. It turns short clips the owner films each day into a short video and publishes it to the owner's own accounts. These terms cover its use.

Who can use it

daily-journal is not offered to the public. Only its owner signs in to it, and only with the owner's own YouTube and TikTok accounts.

What it does

  • YouTube: uploads the day's video to the owner's channel, schedules when it goes public and adds it to a monthly playlist.
  • TikTok: sends the same video to the owner's TikTok inbox as a draft. It never posts to TikTok by itself: the owner reviews the draft, adds the caption and chooses who can see it in the TikTok app before posting.

All videos are filmed by the owner, and the owner is responsible for them. Music in the videos is royalty-free and used under its licence. Uploads must follow the YouTube Terms of Service, the TikTok Terms of Service and the TikTok Community Guidelines.
