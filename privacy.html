Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

daily-journal is a personal, single-user tool run by its owner on their own home server.

What it accesses

With the owner's consent it uses two Google permissions: youtube.upload, to upload the owner's own journal videos to the owner's own YouTube channel, and youtube, to set their thumbnails and add them to the channel's monthly playlists. It does not read, change or delete any other YouTube or Google data.

On TikTok it uses two permissions: user.info.basic, which TikTok requires for sign-in, and video.upload, to send the same videos to the owner's TikTok inbox as drafts. It never posts to TikTok by itself, and it does not read the owner's TikTok profile, followers, videos or messages.

What it stores

The Google and TikTok sign-in tokens and the owner's video clips are stored only on the owner's home server. Clips are deleted after they have been uploaded. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.

Google API Services

daily-journal's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

TikTok

Use of TikTok follows the TikTok Terms of Service and TikTok Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time: for Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions, for TikTok in the app under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.
