Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

daily-journal is a personal, single-user tool run by its owner on their own home server.

What it accesses

With the owner's consent it uses one Google permission, youtube.upload, to upload the owner's own journal videos to the owner's own YouTube channel. It does not read, change or delete any other YouTube or Google data.

What it stores

The OAuth refresh token and the owner's video clips are stored only on the owner's home server. Clips are deleted after they have been uploaded. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.

Google API Services

daily-journal's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
